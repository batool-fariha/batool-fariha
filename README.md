## Fariha Batool

I build products where the data has to be right, because someone downstream is going to act on it.

The question I keep coming back to is simple: *where does this go wrong, and can we catch it sooner?* I first asked it doing household surveys in Karachi. I asked it again owning a health platform used by more than 1.5 million people. Now I ask it about whatever I'm curious about at the moment.

I'm a product manager by trade and a researcher by training. I also build my own prototypes, because sometimes the fastest way to understand a problem is to make something and watch it break.

**Currently:** Master's in Health Sciences at the University of Twente, in Enschede.

### Background

I spent three years at the **Gates Foundation** as a Product Associate, owning a mobile health platform from roadmap to release. A lot of that work was quiet: tracing bad records back to the form that let them in, and writing privacy into features before they were built rather than after.

Before that I worked in research, on water insecurity at the **Karachi Water Project** and health policy at **Aahung**. That's where I learned that most data problems begin long before the spreadsheet.
