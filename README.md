Product Manager in HealthTech. I've shipped products used by **1.5M+ people**, and I'm doing an MSc in Health Sciences at the University of Twente.

I build small, working tools around problems I keep running into, whether in product work or in digital health. Every project below has a live demo you can try in your browser.

---

#### 🩺 Health & wearables

**[smartwatch-polygraph](https://github.com/batool-fariha/smartwatch-polygraph)** · [live demo](https://smartwatch-polygraph.vercel.app)
Is your smartwatch lying to you? Upload an Apple Health export and it scores where your sleep, steps and heart rate contradict each other. Everything runs in your browser, so your data never leaves your device.

**[carechaos](https://github.com/batool-fariha/carechaos)** · [live demo](https://carechaos.vercel.app)
Chaos engineering for remote patient monitoring, in simulation. Break a fictional pipeline on purpose and check whether alerts still reach the clinician.

#### 🧭 Product management

**[prd-doctor](https://github.com/batool-fariha/prd-doctor)** · [live demo](https://prd-doctor.vercel.app)
Scores your PRD from 0 to 100 across 12 categories, with suggested fixes that quote your own text.

**[pm-arena](https://github.com/batool-fariha/pm-arena)** · [live demo](https://pm-arena-phi.vercel.app)
Practise PM interview cases against the clock, by voice or text, and get a heuristic critique of your answer.
